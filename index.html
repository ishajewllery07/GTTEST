Privacy Policy

🔒

Your Privacy Matters

GTEST – Student Learning App

Last Updated: 20 November 2026

1. Information We Collect

GTEST may store student account details and information entered while using the app, such as profile information, study notes and learning progress, depending on the features used.

2. Firebase Database

GTEST uses Firebase Realtime Database to store and retrieve student account information. Data may also be cached in the browser's local storage to support app functionality.

3. Local Storage

Some information may be stored locally in your browser or device. Clearing browser data may remove locally stored information.

4. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide student login, profile management and the learning features available in GTEST.

5. Data Security

We aim to protect information used by GTEST. However, no online storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Users should keep their login details private.

6. Data Sharing

Information may be processed by Firebase as part of providing the app's database services. We do not claim that data is never shared with service providers.

7. Children's Privacy

GTEST is designed for students. Students should use the app with appropriate guidance from a parent, guardian or teacher.
